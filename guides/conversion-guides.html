Practical Conversion Guides

📅 Updated: January 2026 📖 6 min read

This guide provides practical conversion techniques for the most common unit conversions. Use these as a quick reference or a learning tool.

Length Conversions

  • CM to Inches: Divide by 2.54
  • Inches to CM: Multiply by 2.54
  • KM to Miles: Multiply by 0.621371
  • Miles to KM: Multiply by 1.60934
  • Meters to Feet: Multiply by 3.28084

Weight Conversions

  • KG to LBS: Multiply by 2.20462
  • LBS to KG: Divide by 2.20462
  • Ounces to Grams: Multiply by 28.3495
  • Grams to Ounces: Divide by 28.3495

Temperature Conversions

  • Celsius to Fahrenheit: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
  • Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

Volume Conversions

  • Liters to Gallons (US): Multiply by 0.264172
  • Gallons (US) to Liters: Multiply by 3.78541
  • Milliliters to Cups: Divide by 236.588
