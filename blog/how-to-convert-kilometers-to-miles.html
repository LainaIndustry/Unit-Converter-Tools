How to Convert Kilometers to Miles

📅 Updated: January 2026 📖 4 min read

Converting kilometers to miles is a common need for travelers, runners, and anyone dealing with distances in different measurement systems. This guide will show you how to make this conversion quickly and accurately.

The Formula

Miles = Kilometers × 0.621371

To convert kilometers to miles, simply multiply the number of kilometers by 0.621371.

Step-by-Step Examples

Example 1: Converting 10 km to miles

10 km × 0.621371 = 6.21371 miles

Example 2: Converting 42.195 km (marathon) to miles

42.195 km × 0.621371 = 26.2188 miles

Example 3: Converting 100 km to miles

100 km × 0.621371 = 62.1371 miles

How to Convert Miles to Kilometers

To convert miles to kilometers, use the reverse formula:

Kilometers = Miles × 1.60934
