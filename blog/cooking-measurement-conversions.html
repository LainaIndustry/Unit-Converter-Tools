A Guide to Cooking Measurement Conversions

📅 Updated: January 2026 📖 5 min read

Cooking often requires converting measurements between different units, especially when following recipes from different countries. This guide provides essential conversion charts for the kitchen.

Volume Conversions

Volume measurements are common in cooking, especially for liquids and dry ingredients.

Common Ingredient Conversions

  • Flour: 1 cup = 120 g
  • Sugar: 1 cup = 200 g
  • Butter: 1 stick = 113 g = ½ cup
  • Honey: 1 cup = 340 g
  • Milk: 1 cup = 240 ml
