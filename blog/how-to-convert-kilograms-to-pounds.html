How to Convert Kilograms to Pounds

📅 Updated: January 2026 📖 4 min read

Converting kilograms to pounds is one of the most common weight conversions, especially for fitness, cooking, and international travel. This guide will show you how to make this conversion quickly and accurately.

The Formula

Pounds = Kilograms × 2.20462

To convert kilograms to pounds, simply multiply the number of kilograms by 2.20462.

Step-by-Step Examples

Example 1: Converting 1 kg to pounds

1 kg × 2.20462 = 2.20462 lb

Example 2: Converting 5 kg to pounds

5 kg × 2.20462 = 11.0231 lb

Example 3: Converting 70 kg to pounds

70 kg × 2.20462 = 154.3234 lb

How to Convert Pounds to Kilograms

To convert pounds to kilograms, use the reverse formula:

Kilograms = Pounds ÷ 2.20462

Practical Applications

  • Fitness: Tracking body weight
  • Travel: Luggage weight limits
  • Cooking: Following international recipes
  • Shipping: Calculating shipping weights
