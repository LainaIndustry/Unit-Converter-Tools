Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

ConvertSphere ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Information Collection

We do not collect or store personal information unless you voluntarily provide it through our contact form or other communication channels.

Cookies

We use cookies to remember your theme preference (dark/light mode) and to store your recent conversions locally in your browser. These are stored on your device and are not sent to our servers.

Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how visitors use our site. These services may collect anonymous usage data. You can opt out by disabling cookies in your browser settings.

Advertising

We may display advertisements through Google AdSense or similar services. These services may use cookies to serve relevant ads. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-Party Services

Our site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these sites.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal information we may hold about you. Please contact us through our contact page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page.
